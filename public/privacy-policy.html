Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

About the app

Word Circle is a word puzzle game developed by Akarshan. You can play without creating an account. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Information and advertising

The app displays ads using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app activity, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to your settings and applicable consent requirements. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's data practices.

Word Circle does not use this website to collect game data. The website host may process standard request information, such as IP address and access logs, to operate and protect the service.

Data choices

You can manage your device's advertising and privacy settings. Removing the app deletes game progress stored locally on that device. Ad-related information may be managed through Google's settings and policies.

Children

Word Circle is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have a concern about information associated with a child, contact the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
